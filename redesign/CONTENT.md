## what is new, and what it is

- **Figure 1 is a real recording.** 260 s of CHB-MIT chb01_03 (8 bipolar channels, 2860–3120 s, the annotated seizure
  2996–3036 s), fetched by HTTP range from PhysioNet by `scripts/eeg.mjs`, filtered and stored as `data/eeg.bin`.
  Labelled **recorded**, with licence (ODC-By 1.0) and citation.
- **Figure 2's inferred states** are the engine's own (`data/instrument.json`); labelled **inferred**.
- **Figure 2's trace** is the engine's Wilson–Cowan equations (`upstream/research/backend/cortex2/neural.py`),
  integrated in the page with its own seeded noise; labelled **simulated**.
- **Plates** (fullscreen video, ink drawings) are generated by fal and say so in their captions. None shows a person,
  a patient, a real recording or a result.

## the deck and the team video
